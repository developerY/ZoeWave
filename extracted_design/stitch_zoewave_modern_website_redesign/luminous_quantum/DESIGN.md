## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an exhilarating, enterprise-scale deep-tech visual identity. Designed for a forward-looking parent holding company, it fuses high-velocity capital momentum with cutting-edge physical sciences and engineering. The atmosphere is unapologetically luminous, pure, and electric—eschewing dark, dystopian tropes in favor of bright, pristine optimism.

The design movement bridges **Precision Modernism** and **Crystalline Glassmorphism**:
- **Pure Canvas:** Clean, radiant white and pale-slate backdrops create an expansive laboratory-grade field.
- **Kinetic Light:** Prismatic, high-saturation accents—electric cyan, vivid violet, and laser azure—inject kinetic charge without cluttering workflows.
- **Enterprise Gravitas:** Deep iridescent navy grounding elements anchor high-stakes portfolio data, governance surfaces, and executive analytics.
- **Atmosphere:** Razor-sharp crystalline panels, delicate refractive perimeter borders, and ambient light leaks convey structural mastery and deep-tech innovation.

## Colors

The palette is tuned for high-contrast clarity, crystalline radiance, and vivid energy across daylight and studio monitors:

- **Primary (`#0099ff` — Electric Azure / Cerulean):** The core vector of action, interactive elements, focused nodes, and live signal indicators.
- **Secondary (`#1e3a8a` — Iridescent Ultramarine):** The foundational enterprise structural anchor used for bold typography, high-value visual groupings, and institutional framing.
- **Tertiary (`#6366f1` — Electric Violet):** Reserved for multi-tier holding data, breakthrough venture markers, and crystalline gradients paired with `#06b6d4` (Cyan).
- **Neutral (`#0f172a` — Deep Space Slate):** Yields crisp, AAA-grade typographic readability on brilliant pure backgrounds.
- **Surfaces & Tones:**
  - `surface-canvas`: `#ffffff`
  - `surface-subtle`: `#f8fafc`
  - `surface-card`: `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` with backdrop-filter blur
  - `surface-container-high`: `#f1f5f9`
  - `border-glass`: `rgba(0, 153, 255, 0.16)` and `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`

## Typography

The typographic system contrasts structural, hyper-modern technological display letterforms with high-efficiency text legibility:

- **Space Grotesk (Headlines & Labels):** Provides an architectural, scientific cadence. Numbers and acronyms present an authentic deep-tech signature. Labels in `label-sm` leverage uppercase tracking for hardware-grade telemetries.
- **Inter (Body & Content):** Delivers uninterrupted readability across data-dense parent tables, financial summaries, and governance documents.
- **Numerical Treatment:** Tabular figures are enforced across financial projections, holding percentages, and telemetry dashboards to maintain rigorous visual alignment.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an airy, hyper-clean 12-column fluid grid system engineered to breathe effortless authority:

- **Desktop (≥ 1280px):** 12-column layout with `gutter-lg` (2rem) and `margin-desktop` (3rem), maximizing negative space around primary venture cards and metric pods. Max content constraint caps at 1440px for focus.
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with `gutter` (1.5rem) and `margin` (2rem). Complex portfolio grids compress into dual-column cards.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout utilizing `gutter-sm` (1rem) and `margin-mobile` (1rem). High-priority metrics convert to horizontal scroll tracks with snap positions.
- **Spatial Rhythm:** Built on an 8pt base grid. Padding tokens (`space-xs` to `space-xl`) govern atomic separation, ensuring that component internal space remains distinct from outer layout structural framing.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a crystalline glassmorphic layering strategy combined with ambient prismatic light dispersion rather than muddy drop shadows:

- **Level 0 (Canvas):** Pure `#ffffff` or clean geometric micro-mesh gradient shifts over `#f8fafc`.
- **Level 1 (Sub-Panels & Base Cards):** Translucent porcelain layer (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` to `#ffffff`), bound by a 1px border of `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`, with an ultra-diffused atmospheric glow: `0 8px 30px rgba(0, 153, 255, 0.04)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Hubs & Portals):** Pure white backplate with a crystalline edge of `rgba(0, 153, 255, 0.20)` and dual layered illumination: `0 12px 36px rgba(30, 58, 138, 0.06), 0 2px 6px rgba(0, 153, 255, 0.08)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals, Overlays, Floating Nav):** Glassmorphic backplate (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)`, `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`), framed with a luminous hairline gradient border (`linear-gradient(135deg, rgba(0, 153, 255, 0.4), rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.1))`), elevated by `0 24px 60px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates geometric precision balanced with friendly technical sophistication. The base curvature token is set to `2` (Rounded):

- **Inputs, Chips, and Buttons:** Standardized to `0.5rem` (8px) corner radius, providing crisp precision without harsh industrial rigidity.
- **Enterprise Cards and Data Panels:** Leverage `1rem` (16px / `rounded-lg`) to contain high-density metrics gracefully.
- **Featured Venture Nodes and Modals:** Scale up to `1.5rem` (24px / `rounded-xl`), creating inviting macro containers for deep-tech storytelling and cross-entity matrices.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Electric:** Solid `#0099ff` background with crisp `#ffffff` text, transitioning on hover to `#0284c7` accompanied by an electric halo glow (`box-shadow: 0 0 20px rgba(0, 153, 255, 0.35)`).
- **Secondary Corporate:** Deep ultramarine `#1e3a8a` fill with pure white typography for high-authority confirmations.
- **Glass / Ghost:** Translucent background (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`), crystalline 1px perimeter border (`rgba(0, 153, 255, 0.2)`), `#0f172a` text. On hover, background shifts to `#f1f5f9` with vivid text highlight.

### Chips & Telemetry Tags
- Compact tags with `0.5rem` radius, typed in uppercase `label-sm` (Space Grotesk).
- Status variants utilize luminous tint fills: Electric Cyan tint (`rgba(6, 182, 212, 0.1)`) with `#0284c7` text; Violet tint (`rgba(99, 102, 241, 0.1)`) with `#6366f1` text. Includes an active pulsing 6px radial signal pip.

### Cards & Venture Hubs
- Rendered on Level 1 crystalline bases with `1rem` corner rounding.
- Hover states initiate a micro-lift (`translateY(-2px)`) while the perimeter border transitions smoothly from neutral slate-tinted hair to vivid cyan-cerulean iridescence.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Inputs:** Minimalist `#ffffff` containers with `1px` subtle outline (`#e2e8f0`). When focused, transitions to an energetic `1.5px` border of `#0099ff` complemented by a soft 4px aura ring (`rgba(0, 153, 255, 0.15)`).
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Sharp squircle geometry with active fills in `#0099ff`, embedding crisp white check icons.

### Lists & Matrix Tables
- Alternating subtle rows (`#ffffff` to `#f8fafc`) intersected with 1px border dividers (`#f1f5f9`).
- Row hover triggers an energetic light wash across the cursor line (`rgba(0, 153, 255, 0.03)`).

### Specialized Domain Components: Quantum Telemetry Gauge & Subsidiary Pill
- **Telemetry Gauge:** Clean radial vector dials visualizing capital deployment and deep-tech pipeline velocity with dynamic `#0099ff` to `#6366f1` gradient strokes.
- **Subsidiary Pill:** Multi-entity brand badge pairing holding hierarchy labels with iridescent venture accents.
